# Impact Report — Sample

An anonymised example of Impactable's quarterly Impact Report, built on the locked v1.1 information architecture.
Ten tabs, static HTML, no build step. Client shown as **Northwind Commerce**, a fictional marketplace growth agency.

## What this demonstrates
| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Summary | Thesis first, scorecard, what is working and what is not |
| Results | Account funnel and movement inside the period |
| Performance | **The single home for every spend number.** Windsor verified ledger |
| Intent | ICP filtered website identification, cross referenced against ad exposure |
| Pipeline | Reverse funnel and three tier attribution, click verified through to ecosystem |
| Insights | Audience segments and messaging and creative |
| Ecosystem | The 22 motion Board, maturity score, next three unlocks selected by the laws |
| Next 90 | Typed recommendations, budget arc, commitment log |
| Strategy | ICP, competitive white space, assumptions ledger, hard coded definitions |
| Sources | Every input with its limitation, what cannot be claimed, corrections log |

## What has been anonymised
- Client name, domain and tenant identifier
- All client and agency individuals, replaced with roles
- **All identified individuals from visitor data, replaced with Contact 1..n**
- All prospect and enterprise account names, replaced with ICP Brand 1..n and Enterprise Account 1..n
- All competitor names, replaced with Competitor 1..n
- All email addresses and domains

## What has been kept, and why
Spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, penetration, frequency and maturity scores are unchanged, because the point of the example is to show how the numbers are handled: one home per number, verdicts judged by funnel job, absence reported as absence, and corrections logged openly.

## Residual identification risk
The category (marketplace growth agency), the reporting window and the spend figures remain real. Anyone with independent knowledge of the account could narrow it down. **If this is going to prospects rather than internal use, shift the dates by a quarter and round the spend figures.**
