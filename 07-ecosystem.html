Next three unlocks

Selected by the six laws, not by preference. Two free moves rank above the paid expansion.

Next 1 / Law 1 / $0

C14 Enrichment: activate the warm pools

20,000+ newsletter subscribers, 1,325 identified companies and every CRM contact have never been uploaded as matched audiences. One afternoon. Refills the audience the enterprise exclusions shrank and lifts penetration without new spend.

Next 2 / Law 2 / $0

C11 Conversion tracking to systematic

Campaign Manager says 5, DemandSense 0, Google 2, and no deal outcome exists. $18,406 of Windsor verified spend cannot be tied to revenue. Unlocks C13 account intel and makes C15 measurable.

Next 3 / Law 5 / $2,500

C15 Meta B2B, the gated unlock

Earned rather than asserted. The LGF campaign is validated at 18 leads and $170.76 CPL but reaches 47.6% of its pool. 4,036 unreached inside it, 24,081 across the ABM and 180D pools. Activate stays gated until C11 and C14 clear.

02

What the Board says

Strength

Create carries the account at 14.6 of 25

Content, a 20,000 subscriber newsletter and thought leader ads all running. This is the pillar most agencies never build, and it is why the paid programme has anything warm to work with.

Weakness

Convert scores 7.8 of 20 across six motions

BOF conversion and sales activation are both at zero depth. The system generates warm accounts and then has nothing to close them with. This is the structural reason engagement does not become pipeline.

Gated

Activate is unscored by design

Meta, programmatic and outreach do not count toward maturity until the signal layer supports them. Scoring them would reward launching channels before they can be measured, which is the failure this account already made on Google.
