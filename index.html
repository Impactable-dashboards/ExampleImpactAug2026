1Executive Summary

Google Ads — July 1–31, 2026 · $22,400 budget

July was the account's best month to date. The account spent $22,453 (slightly over budget due to auction variance in Week 1) across two Search campaigns, driving 2,431 clicks at a blended CTR of 9.03% and blended CPC of $9.24. The account produced 92 confirmed conversions at a $243 cost-per-lead, within the $250 target. Search impression share climbed to 54% from 31% in June following bid strategy changes and landing page improvements. The Retail Partner service line remains the efficiency leader ($8.53 CPC, $211 CPL), while Marketplace Services improved after 38 negative keywords were added mid-month. Six broad-match search terms are flagged as August optimization targets.

✅ Win: 92 conversions at $243 CPL — on target for B2B. Conversion volume up 31% vs. June.
✅ Win: Search impression share grew from 31% to 54% MoM after bid strategy switch to Target CPA.
🔧 Opportunity: 6 broad-match terms spending $2,847 combined at $13–$18 CPC with 3 total conversions — prime candidates for match-type tightening.

2Account Activity & Optimizations

Changes and observations during July 2026

  • Switched to Target CPA bidding at $235 target — July 8: Moved both campaigns from Maximize Clicks to Target CPA after reaching the minimum 30-conversion threshold. Impression share improved immediately; CPC dropped $1.84 within the following week.
  • Negative keyword expansion — July 14 (38 negatives added): Search term review on the Marketplace Services ad group revealed a cluster of off-ICP queries around consumer-facing e-commerce tools. Added 38 negatives across the campaign. CPC in that group dropped from $14.62 to $11.09 in the second half of the month.
  • Search impression share climbed to 54%: Up from 31% in June, driven by the CPA bid switch and Quality Score improvements from landing page copy alignment completed late June. Budget cap is no longer the primary limiter — ranking is now the constraint on roughly 30% of missed impressions.
  • Landing page alignment for Channel Migration ad group: Updated the destination URL for the Channel Migration ad group to a more specific page after identifying a high bounce rate. Conversion volume is still low but improving.
  • Overspend of $53 in Week 1: Auction volatility early in the month pushed spend slightly above the $22,400 budget. Will tighten daily caps in August to stay within budget.
