What this tab shows

59 ICP companies and 30 named ICP buyers are already visiting the site. Three have also been served ads.

Every ICP company in this dataset visits only one to three times, so they sit invisibly beneath high frequency job seekers and crawlers in any volume ranking. This analysis instead pulls the full industry distribution across all 1,306 identified companies, sweeps every ICP industry bucket exhaustively, and filters people by seniority and department rather than by session count.

The result is the buyer list below: 59 ICP companies, 30 named ICP people, and 3 companies already touched by paid. ICP Brand 51 is the clearest overlap — present in both the July ad cohort and the website, with two named marketing managers.

The account to lead with

ICP Brand 51 is the only complete account in the dataset: ads, then site, then two named buyers.

246 paid impressions and 7 clicks in the 180 day window, present in the July engaged cohort at 179 impressions, 2 website visits, and two named people in performance marketing and brand management. 501 to 1,000 employees, squarely inside the ICP band, cosmetics.

Every other account in this report has one or two of those three signals. This is the single proof that the full funnel can work, and it is the strongest named account story in the quarter.

01

Method, stated so it can be challenged

Companies
Full industry distribution across all 1,306 identified companies, then exhaustive pulls of the 11 core ICP industries (74 companies, complete set) plus a sweep of the mislabel prone buckets Automotive, Building materials, Wholesale, Consumer services and Design (100 rows).
People, pass A
Seniority filter. CXO returned 95 rows, the complete set. Director, VP, Owner, Partner and Senior returned 100 rows.
People, pass B
Department filter across Marketing, Purchasing and Product management at all seniorities, 57 rows, the complete set. This catches manager level brand and ecommerce people that a seniority filter misses.
Join
Normalised on lowercase, punctuation stripped, corporate suffixes removed (inc, llc, group, usa, brands, the, company), minimum token length 5 to avoid short string false positives.
Label override
WebID industry labels were disregarded where wrong. ICP Brand 51, ICP Brand 47, ICP Brand 10, ICP Brand 110, ICP Brand 111 and ICP Brand 92 are all filed under Automotive. ICP Brand 80, ICP Brand 18, ICP Brand 59 and ICP Brand 35 are under Building materials.
Known limit
The ad exposure list is the top 100 ICP industry companies by impressions, which bottoms out around 235 impressions. Companies below that threshold may have exposure this join cannot see. ICP Brand 9 is a confirmed example at 145 July impressions. So 3 matched is a floor and 56 unmatched is a ceiling.

What to do

1 / this week

Upload the 7 Tier 1 accounts as a matched audience

ICP Brand 51, ICP Brand 18, ICP Brand 88, ICP Brand 112, ICP Brand 100, ICP Brand 59, ICP Brand 52. Six of the seven have never received a paid impression. Named buyer, right size, right function, demonstrated intent.

2 / this week

Build the wider ICP list into a second audience

The remaining 22 ICP accounts plus the 56 ICP companies without a resolved person. Small, cheap, and currently receiving nothing.

3 / this week

Exclude the 11 competitor entities

Two of them, Competitor 27 and Competitor 1, sit in the very high engagement tier. Excluding them keeps the ABM audience clean and the engagement numbers honest, even though the headline count steps down slightly.

4 / before outreach

Enrich the four unknown sizes and verify every URL

ICP Brand 49, ICP Brand 21, ICP Brand 33 and ICP Brand 26 have CEO or owner level visitors and no size data, so they cannot be qualified against the contract floor. Verify all LinkedIn URLs by hand.

5 / next 30 days

Attack the 201 to 500 conversion gap directly

Seven of the 30 named ICP buyers sit in this band, and the audience analysis shows the same band delivering 319 clicks at a healthy CTR with zero leads. The audience is right and engaging on both channels. Fix the offer and the landing experience for this band specifically.

6 / the pattern to remember

The best buyers visit quietly, once or twice

ICP buyers do not announce themselves with volume. They arrive once or twice and sit beneath the careers-page noise, which is exactly why they are identified here by industry, seniority and department rather than by traffic.
